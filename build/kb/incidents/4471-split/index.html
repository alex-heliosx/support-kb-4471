5 reports read as a one-day move; filed under parser by tenant.

  1. Parser reads dates month first (ticket #1): the parser row, counted as the view counts it, the footnoted one-day moves included. Every tenant on file writes the day first. A date with a day of 12 or less comes out with day and month swapped. Fix: parse in the tenant’s locale.
  2. booking-v3 form hints MM/DD (ticket #2): the hint row. On day-first tenants that run booking-v3, the form prints MM/DD/YYYY. People type month first, the form reads day first, and the date flips. Only the form channel of those tenants. Fixing the parser does not fix this.
  3. Dates move by one day (ticket #4): the five reports the view footnotes under parser. The desk filed them there by tenant and counts them there, so they are inside the parser row too. No swap: the invite lands a day early or late, or shows different days in different cities. A month-first parse cannot produce them, so look at time-zone and day-boundary handling.
