5 reports read as a one-day move; filed under parser by tenant.

  1. Parser reads dates month first (ticket #1): every report causeOf ties to parser. Every tenant on file writes the day first. A date with a day of 12 or less comes out with day and month swapped. Fix: parse in the tenant’s locale.
  2. booking-v3 form hints MM/DD (ticket #2): every report causeOf ties to hint. On day-first tenants that run booking-v3, the form prints MM/DD/YYYY. People type month first, the form reads day first, and the date flips. Only the form channel of those tenants. Fixing the parser does not fix this. The five reports the desk noted as one-day moves (desk-notes.json) are a reading by people, not a cause the code knows: causeOf ties them to the parser by tenant, so they sit in ticket #1 and are named there. No swap: a day early or late, so check time-zone and day-boundary handling when the parser fix lands.

How they were told apart

  • The cause depends on the tenant, its locale and its booking form (src/lib/kb/causes.ts), not on the subject line and not on intake’s tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and it is wrong often: compare “tagged as” with “cause” in the table above.
  • The export pages overlap, so one report can appear twice. Count by id.
  • One-day moves come from desk-notes.json (oneDayMove).

For the desk

  • Match on cause, not on subject. Two reports with the same subject can have different causes, and the reverse.
  • When a report does not fit the cause its tenant points to, say so in the ticket the code puts it in, and give it its own ticket once the code can tell it apart.
